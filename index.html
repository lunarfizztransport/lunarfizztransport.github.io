Drum Groove Tool

Drum Groove Tool

Record yourself playing drums, see exactly how your timing sits, and get coaching plus new grooves and fills in your style.

Demo video coming soon

You play along to a click. The tool finds every hit and measures:

  • whether you rush or drag
  • how consistent you are
  • which drum is early or late
  • how much you swing

Then it gives feedback like a drum teacher would, with exercises and groove and fill ideas to learn from and make your own. Every idea comes as a MIDI file you can play in GarageBand.

It runs on your computer (Python) and works with a mic, phone recordings, or an electronic kit over USB.

About.txt

Richelle Guice

This is where I keep my collages and side projects.

Sometimes I go into a collage with an idea. Other times I see something that calls to me, and a strong idea forms from there.

Then I go digging through random scraps: old National Geographics, stray photos, whatever paper I've got. None of it was meant to go together, and the fun part is getting unrelated images to mean something in a weird, fantastical way. Stuff changes along the way, with lots of redoing and undoing. But the idea stays, and that's where the inspiration comes from.

Prints and commissions are open. Message me on Instagram at @lunarfizztransport.
