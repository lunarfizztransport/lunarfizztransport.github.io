About

I make collages by hand from National Geographic back issues and whatever magazines turn up at thrift stores and library sales. Each piece starts with one image I can't stop looking at, then gets built out with scissors, an X-Acto knife and a lot of glue stick.

Prints and commissions are open. Message me on Instagram: @your.handle
