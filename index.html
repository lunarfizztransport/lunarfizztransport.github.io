Made by hand.
On purpose.

I'm Richelle Guice. I make collages out of old National Geographics and basically any photo or scrap of paper that looks at me funny. Each one starts with a single image I can't stop looking at, then gets built out with scissors, an X-Acto knife and a lot of glue stick.

Prints and commissions are open. Send me a message on Instagram at @lunarfizztransport

All pieces are physical, cut-paper originals.
