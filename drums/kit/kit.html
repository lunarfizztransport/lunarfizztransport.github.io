How the tool uses this

You played a few hits on each drum. The tool measured which frequencies each one adds, from the low thump of the kick to the high sizzle of the hi-hat. When it analyzes a take, it explains every hit as a mix of these learned sounds, so a kick and hi-hat played together come out as both. Because it learns your drums on your mic in your room, it copes with sound that a fixed rule would get wrong.
